# proof360 — why

*Written for the people who use it. How it's built is `README.md`; what changed is
`docs/CHANGELOG-SARVESH.md`.*

## For the founder

You turn up with whatever you already have. A website. A pitch deck. A paragraph you
wrote at midnight. A firehose of links. You don't fill in a form.

It reads what you gave it and shows you **a twin of you and your idea** — what it worked
out about you, and, separately, what it's only guessing. Guesses are labelled as guesses.

Then you correct it. What you say outranks what it inferred, permanently, and it stops
asking about that thing. The twin is yours; you can see everything in it and change
anything in it.

Two moments this is really built around:

**When you add something to your shortlist**, it saves the thing *and the reason you added
it* — the moment you were in when it made sense. Six weeks later "add Vanta" isn't a naked
line item you can't account for. You can see what you were looking at.

**While you're not here**, it goes and fills in what's missing, and brings back where it
got each piece.

You never have to log in to see your own read. If you must register before you learn
anything, it isn't a diagnosis, it's a lead form.

## For the investor

The same record, seen differently — not a second document the founder had to prepare.

You can tell, per claim, what the founder said in their own words, what the system
inferred, and what's been corrected since. Sources are attached, so you can check
something without asking the founder for another PDF.

There's no score to argue with. The point isn't to rank the founder — it's to make the
crossing between you and them cheaper, and to remove the part where diligence is thirty
emails asking for evidence that already exists.

## The money side

Alongside the twin it keeps the record of engagements — which suppliers the founder
actually spoke to, and what came of it. The diagnosis is the front door. The routing is
the business.

## Where the richness comes from

The twin gets deeper because CORPUS is underneath it, and CORPUS carries sources. A claim
in your record can point at the document it came from, and you can follow it.

## What we deliberately don't do

**We don't grade.** Hold a lamp so the founder can see the next few steps and choose one.
Rank people and they get defensive, and a defensive founder tells you nothing true.

**We don't invent numbers — live or illustrative.** If there's no measurement behind a
row, the row doesn't exist. This bit was learned the hard way.

**We don't hide the read behind a login.**
